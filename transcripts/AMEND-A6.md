## Amendment A6: a vetoed window ends (22 November 2026)

Counsel's text. Recorded by `amend` A6 under SPEC §7 after a `decision` of kind `transmute` naming it, in force
from its `activate_at`. Appended; overrides A5.1 and any brief where they conflict. Drafted on his answer 12 of
20 November ("The one-clause early-end amendment (a window also ends, as failed, at his veto) proceeds now";
`transcripts/RULING-20261120-promotion-judge.md`) and answer 17 there. Forced by the record: his veto of
C2a-prime (seq 5879, 2026-11-18T12:01:17Z) refuses its criterion 7 and ends nothing, because A5.1 names only two
ends, the stated end and a `promote`. So C2a-prime, which can no longer be promoted (COUNSEL-OPEN row 69), holds
until 2026-11-30T00:00:00Z every bar its brief sets inside the window, "no second push inside the window"
(`transcripts/BRIEF-C2a-prime-draft.md`, refusal list) among them, and the push that moves the promotion
evaluator under `judge/`, which the ruling of 20 November puts before that date, has no lawful place.

1. **A vetoed window ends.** A window also ends, as failed (§6, A3.2), at a `decision` entry of kind `veto` by
   the sovereign whose `subject` names it, as A5.1's naming is read for that window, and whose `seq` is at or
   above this amendment's `activate_at`. Nothing else about the window changes: its stated end is not moved,
   and every act that the charter, a brief or a ruling keys to a failed window's stated end stays keyed to it,
   among them the fix of the window (the ruling of 4 November, conditions 2 and 3) and its promotion forecast's
   `resolves_by` (A5.2).
2. **Witnesses** (§2, maxim 2). Violated by: a `push` entered after such a veto and before the window's stated
   end, read as a push inside the window. Satisfied by: C2a-prime, vetoed again at or above this amendment's
   height, and then the push that moves the promotion evaluator, which opens nothing (the ruling of 20 November,
   answers 5 and 17). Not reached: the veto at seq 5879, entered before this amendment (A2.7: never over earlier
   entries). It still refuses C2a-prime's criterion 7, and it ends nothing.
3. **Price and order.** A6 is the sixth amendment counting A1, the fifth entered by `amend` (seqs 4, 30, 67 and
   137), and the first since A5's of 30 October. §12's guard applies; C2a-prime's A3.4 line reads `procedure 281 : object 1152`
   at seq 6997.
   - **What it departs from, for this one clause.** (a) His principle 5 of 5 November: "The sovereign's reserved
     acts are the irreversible and the change entry; executing a mechanical fact — by veto, or by instructing a
     resolution — is not one of them" (`transcripts/A6-CASE-20261105.md`). Here his veto ends a window, and the
     end is his act, not the record's. The other form, the clause of 5 November under which a window ends at
     the first prefix on which its tool prints `failed: criterion k cannot hold`, stays a C2c item (COUNSEL-OPEN
     row 39) and takes another id. (b) COUNSEL-OPEN row 28 (no charter rule that has not lived in a brief) and
     row 33's procedure, which row 69 named for this clause at C2c-3. Its readers are named by counsel, not
     elected, and read the text and the record without counsel's argument; it carries the forecast below; the
     §12 count is stated, and no budget was set before it. (c) RUNBOOK's order, under which an amendment's
     mechanisms reach `main` before its `amend`; A2 is the precedent for recording first.
   - **What reads it.** Nothing must be built for it to bind: a `veto` is a `decision` the verbs and checkers
     admit today. Until the promotion evaluator's next change, `judge/status.py` reads every window to its stated
     end, and where it and this clause differ, this clause governs. That change reads the end, so that a window's
     items and the wealth it carries (A4.1) stop at such a veto, with a pin
     `vetoed_window_continues`. Mutant: a veto at or above the height is ignored as an end. Twins: such a veto ends
     the window there; a veto below the height ends nothing. Size: about 12 lines of code, 25 of tests and 30 of
     pin, under the replay table that change carries, with the marks his grant gives it.

     V7 are decidable 0 already: V5 by the veto at seq 5879, and V7 with no `promote` within 48 hours of the
