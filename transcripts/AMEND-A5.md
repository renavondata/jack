## Amendment A5: a window is a maximum (30 October 2026)

Counsel's text. Recorded by `amend` A5 under SPEC §7 after a `decision` of kind `transmute` naming it,
in force from its `activate_at`. Appended; overrides §6, A3.2 and any brief where they conflict.
Forced by the record: every pre-stated criterion of C1a held on the prefix of 29 October (PR #41's
`tools/status.py`, run as of 2026-10-29T05:00:00Z), and A3's text — "runs fourteen days" — holds the
window open to 8 November with nothing left for it to measure. The sovereign's budget entry (seq 50)
named fourteen days as a maximum; the text made it a term.

1. **A window is a maximum.** A window named in this charter or in a brief ends at the earlier of its
   stated end and the instant of a `decision` entry by the sovereign whose `subject` names the
   window and whose `kind` is `promote`, which may be entered only once every pre-stated criterion
   of the window holds on the log's current prefix and on the tree at its commit. The exit prefix is
   the log at that instant. A window that reaches its stated end with a criterion unmet has failed
   (§6, A3.2); the stated end may not be moved.
2. **The end is the promotion forecast's date.** From C1b, a window's stated end is the `resolves_by`
   of the promotion forecast the window's brief names, entered before the window opens; a brief
   states no other date.
3. **Price and order.** A5 is the fifth amendment in seventeen days; §12's guard applies and the
   ratio stands at 2.1 : 1 on 30 October. Its forecast is seq 121 (p 0.65 that it is in force by
   8 November). Order: `decision transmute A5`; this `amend` with the default `activate_at`; no
   `push` between the `amend` and its height (SPEC §10.12). One cold read by another family is on
   the record before the `transmute`.
