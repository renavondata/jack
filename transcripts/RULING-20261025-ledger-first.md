# Ruling of 25 October 2026: C1 opens ledger-first

Put to Guy by counsel (`counsel-2`, Claude Fable 5.1) in conversation on 25 October 2026, after
counsel had first claimed, wrongly, that the change needed "no amendment, only a brief". §8's build
order is charter text; a brief may tighten a layer's bounds (A2.4) but may not move content between
layers; the change power is Guy's alone (§3). Hence a ruling.

## The question

jack's charter orders C1 (the clerk and writs; typed acts) before C2 (forecasts under a proper score;
the election). Both repositories have shown the scored record to be the asset, and nothing about the
world can be scored in jack until forecast entries exist. Bring the ledger forward by amendment, or
keep the order and wait a layer?

## Options as put

| | (a) amend §8: C1 = ledger + clerk; C2 = election | (b) keep the order |
|---|---|---|
| first scored world-claim in jack | within C1's first window, ~2 weeks | after C1 and C2, a month or more |
| amendments | a third in eleven days: Fuller's constancy; §12 names procedure growth | none |

| what is scored meanwhile | counsel, from the first week, on the record | counsel, in chat, unscored |

Also named: (c) keep the order and file forecasts in assize's ledger meanwhile (no amendment,
scored today; but assize is freezing, its types are court-internal, and the port is one more
procedure item) — a bridge only if (b); and (d) read §8 narrowly so forecast entries slip into C1
as "the state writs derive from" — refused on principle: counsel settling by drafting what only an
amendment can settle (A2.10).

## Counsel's recommendation and forecasts

(a), with C1 split into two pre-stated windows. Forecasts, to be entered as entries once the types
exist: that (a) is the better choice in hindsight at C1's exit, p = 0.65; that Guy chooses (a),
p = 0.70 (made before the ruling); what would show (a) wrong: C1a's window overrunning fourteen
days, or more than a fifth of its forecasts unresolved past `resolves_by` at the window's end.

## Guy's ruling

Guy asked for the issue and the pros and cons rather than a recommendation alone; having read them,
he chose **(a)**, 25 October 2026 ("I choose (a) anyway"). The ruling is recorded in Amendment A3
(`transcripts/AMEND-A3.md`) and takes effect by `decision transmute A3`, the installing push, and
`amend A3`, in that order.
