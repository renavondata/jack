# The plan read against the literature (26 October 2026)

Counsel's evidence for A4 clauses 2 and 7 and for the C1b brief. A research pass over the
reinforcement-learning, online-learning, game-theoretic-statistics, self-improving-agent and
layered-trust literature, asked for each of the ten rules of the process of 26 October whether the
plan agrees, deviates or is unsupported, and what the correction is. Verdicts and the disposition
taken; the full report with sources is in the conversation record of 26 October and is not law.
