Insider dealing and substantial shareholding disclosures filed with the Hong Kong Exchange under Part XV of the Securities and Futures Ordinance. Track share transactions by directors, chief executives, and substantial shareholders of listed companies. Each record includes the discloser's name, stock code, event date, and extracted details such as event type, number of shares involved, and transaction prices. Covers all forms filed through the HKEX electronic disclosure system.
