HKJC Odds Movements
Racing Pass · Enterprise Free sample 1147.8M rows

Hong Kong horse racing odds history and market movements

The Hong Kong Jockey Club's betting odds as they moved before each race: Win and Place for every runner, and the exotic pools (Quinella, Quinella Place, Forecast, Trio, First Four, Double, and HKJC's top-20 and banker lists for Tierce, Trio, First Four and Quartet). Track how prices changed from the first quote we captured to the close, see which runners attracted late money, and study market efficiency. One row per odds MOVEMENT per combination: each pool update HKJC publishes is stored as the full set of that pool's prices, timestamped with HKJC's own update time (snapshot_timestamp, UTC); repeated unchanged states are not stored, so hold a price flat until the next row. Our collector polls, so if HKJC moves a pool more than once between two polls only the later state is recorded. In the 30 minutes before each race it polled about every minute from 8 December 2021 to 27 September 2023, about every 5 minutes from 1 October 2023 to 28 January 2024, about every minute from 31 January 2024 to 14 July 2024, about every 5 minutes from 1 October 2024 to 27 May 2026 and about every minute since 28 May 2026; earlier in the day, about every 5 minutes until 27 May 2026 and about every minute since 28 May 2026. Post time here is the race card's where we hold it, and otherwise the last five-minute mark at least 30 seconds before betting closed. At 5 minutes before post time the median age of the latest Win price we hold is under a minute in every period; at 1 minute before post time it is under 1, 5, 1, 5 and 1 minutes in those five periods: from 1 October 2023 to 28 January 2024 and from 1 October 2024 to 27 May 2026 polls fell on the five-minute marks post times fall on, so the price 1 minute before post time is usually the one from 5 minutes before. Hong Kong meetings (Sha Tin and Happy Valley): Win and Place odds from 8 December 2021; Quinella, Quinella Place, Forecast, Trio, First Four and Double from 1 October 2024. 18 Hong Kong meetings in that span have no odds at all: 10 September 2023; 3, 6, 10 and 13 March 2024; 8, 11, 15, 18, 22, 25 and 28 September 2024; 3, 6 and 9 November 2024; and 7, 11 and 14 January 2026. 2 more Hong Kong meetings were captured too thinly to time anything before the off, with fewer than 10 Win states in at least half their races: 13 September 2023 and 29 December 2024. Collection stopped while betting was still open at Happy Valley races 3-10 on 26 December 2024 and Sha Tin races 2-10 on 8 June 2025, so those races hold no odds from the last minutes of betting. The final Win odds of every finisher at these 20 meetings and in these 17 races are in our race results dataset (column odds). Elsewhere, missed polls leave 102 other races whose latest Win price at 5 minutes before post time is more than 10 minutes old, 18 of them more than an hour old; the meetings with five or more such races are Happy Valley races 1, 2 and 4-8 on 4 January 2023; Sha Tin races 4-8 on 29 January 2023; Sha Tin races 2 and 7-10 on 19 February 2023; Sha Tin races 1-7 on 28 June 2025; Happy Valley races 1-6 and 8 on 28 January 2026; Happy Valley races 2-4, 6 and 7 on 18 March 2026; and Sha Tin races 2-7 on 29 March 2026. Tierce and Quartet: the Hong Kong Jockey Club publishes no complete odds matrix for either pool, only the 20 lowest-priced combinations (TCETop, QTTTop) and, for each runner taken as banker, the 10 lowest-priced combinations with that runner first (TCEBanker, QTTBanker); Trio and First Four carry the same two lists (TRITop, FFTop, TRIBanker, FFBanker) beside their complete matrices. The top-20 lists run from 1 October 2024 but were not collected for meetings from 1 January 2026 to 24 May 2026; the banker lists exist for 8 meetings between 28 September 2025 and 27 December 2025 and for every meeting from 27 May 2026. Overseas races simulcast in Hong Kong (venues S1-S5) are included from 29 September 2024. Delivered hourly with the rest of the pipeline, not streamed.

Hong Kong Company Address History
PREMIUM Free sample 2.5M rows

Registered-office timeline for Hong Kong companies

A registered-office address timeline for Hong Kong companies. Read it as a history of where a company has been on record, not yet as a current-address lookup. Three of its arms are frozen legacy captures that stopped on 2024-04-18, when the modern e-services API withdrew the address field; a fourth arm reading the Companies Registry's open-data portal resumed observation on 2026-09-03 and is still filling in. RECENCY THEREFORE VARIES BY COMPANY — read last_observed_at per row rather than assuming one as-of date for the dataset. Where the sweep has not yet reached a company, its most recent address may have been superseded by a move we have not seen. Use it to reconstruct where a company was and when it moved; verify against the registry before relying on it for where a company is today. One row per company per distinct registered-office address observed on record; effective_from/effective_to are the registry's own dates where given (else null); first/last_observed_at are our observation bounds. Coverage: 68.4% of active (non-dissolved) Hong Kong companies have at least one address on record here.
