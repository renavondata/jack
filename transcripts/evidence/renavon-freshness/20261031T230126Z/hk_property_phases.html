Development phases: the middle level of the estate→phase→building hierarchy. A phase groups buildings within an estate (e.g. "PHASE 2 CROWN OF ST BARTHS"). The parent estate code and numeric ids, bilingual phase names, the completion-date span of its buildings, a building count, and the phase's centre coordinates and neighbourhood. Join hk_property_buildings.phase_code (members) and, via estate_code, hk_property_estates (parent estate).
