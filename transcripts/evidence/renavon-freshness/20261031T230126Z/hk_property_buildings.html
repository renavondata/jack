Master list of Hong Kong residential buildings from Centaline, a tier of the estate→phase→building→unit hierarchy. English and Chinese estate, phase, building and address names, the parent estate and phase codes, district and county, latitude/longitude, completion date, unit and floor counts, flats per floor, the assigned school net, and a facilities summary. Reconciled across several Centaline sources, newest observation per field.
