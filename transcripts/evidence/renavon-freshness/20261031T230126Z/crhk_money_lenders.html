Hong Kong's official money lender register under the Money Lenders Ordinance (Cap. 163), published by the Companies Registry: current licensees with licence numbers and expiry dates, pending licence applications, expired licences, and dismissed or withdrawn applications — one row per register entry, bilingual names, refreshed weekly from the registry's data files.
