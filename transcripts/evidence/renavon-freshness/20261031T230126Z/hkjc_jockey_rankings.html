hkjc_jockey_rankings

PREMIUM 1,945 rows

Hong Kong jockey standings, wins, and performance statistics

Where does a jockey stand this season, and how did the table move to get there? This dataset keeps the HKJC's jockey standings as a series of snapshots, one per race meeting day, so you can follow wins, win rates and places through a season instead of seeing only today's table. Visiting riders are included, with career and current-season records side by side.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these the current HKJC jockey standings?

They are the HKJC's standings as our collector last read them, delivered hourly, so the current table is in the data together with the history of how it got there. Between our updates, the HKJC's own site is the place to check a standing.

How far back do the standings go?

The series starts at the first standings snapshot we hold, and each race meeting day adds one more, so the history grows through the season. The archive purchase for the jockey standings states its start date in full.

Is there a trainer version?

Yes: HKJC Trainer Rankings has the same layout for trainers.

Can I see the data before paying?

Yes. The sample below shows the latest 6 months of standings with the full set of columns, and you can download it from this page.

What does access cost?

Racing Pass Pro includes the jockey and trainer standings from $149 a month; the archive of jockey standings is a one-time purchase of $149.
