Directory of persons and entities licensed by Hong Kong's Securities and Futures Commission (SFC). Look up any financial intermediary by their Central Entity Reference (CE Ref) and find their name, whether they are an individual or a corporation, the regulated activities they are licensed for (Types 1-12: dealing, advising, asset management, etc.), whether they currently hold an active licence, and whether they hold an Anti-Money Laundering Ordinance (AMLO) licence, and the validity period of their licensing (when it began and, for past licensees, when it ended). Covers all entities on the live SFC public register plus deregistered entities retained from the historical archive. Essential for verifying whether a financial service provider is properly licensed in Hong Kong.
