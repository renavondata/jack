Estate dimension: the TOP of the estate→phase→building→unit hierarchy — the large multi-phase Centaline development (e.g. "Ocean Shores", "Yoho Town"). Bilingual name, the stable numeric id, the building / phase footprint, and the estate-level extras: completion-date range, address, HK1980 grid centre, MTR proximity, the Centaline recent unit-price summary with its active-listing & transaction counts, neighbourhood geography, the Chinese developer legal name, and the primary-market new-launch block (new developments only). Join hk_property_phases / hk_property_buildings / hk_property_transactions on estate_code (or the numeric centa_estate_id). Standalone single-phase developments are intentionally absent — reach those through hk_property_phases.
