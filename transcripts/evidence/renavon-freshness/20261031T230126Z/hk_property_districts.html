Hong Kong district browse/landing dimension: one row per OFFICIAL Hong Kong district (the 18 District Council districts, keyed on Centaline's stable district_code) with bilingual district and region names, its building count and the latest monthly median saleable $/sqft, for top-level navigation and at-a-glance market context. Centaline's two sub-areas (Tseung Kwan O, Lai King / Lai Wan) are folded into their parent districts, so every figure covers the whole district.
