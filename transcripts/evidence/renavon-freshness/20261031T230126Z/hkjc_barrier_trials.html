Hong Kong barrier trial (practice race) results — every horse's run in each morning trial batch at Sha Tin, Conghua and Happy Valley. Includes jockey, trainer, draw, gear, finishing order, time, lengths behind winner (LBW), running positions and the stewards' note on each horse's run. Barrier trials are how unraced and returning horses qualify and show form before the racecard, so this is early-signal data for spotting fitness and intent that the main results don't yet reveal.

gear_label Nullable(String) Equipment decoded to words, e.g. 'Visor (removed) / Blinkers (2nd time) / Tongue Tie'. 'No gear' where the raw code is '--'. Codes are HKJC's own (Equipment Register, racing.hkjc.com/en-us/local/page/reg-approved-gear); a trailing 1/2 means first/second time worn and a trailing '-' means removed this start. Unrecognised codes pass through unchanged. — —
lbw Nullable(String) Lengths behind winner (LBW) — distance behind the first-place finisher. Numeric margins carry an explicit unit suffix, e.g. '2L' (two lengths), '1/2L', '2-1/4L'. Sub-length margins are published as HKJC's abbreviations or words instead of a number: 'Neck', 'Head', 'Nose', 'SH' (short head). HKJC occasionally omits or mis-cases the 'L' at source; the unit is restored here so the numeric form is consistent, and the word margins are passed through as published. — —

batch_split_2_time Nullable(Float64) Second section split time for the batch, in seconds (unpacked from batch_sectional_time). Same for every runner in the batch. — —
