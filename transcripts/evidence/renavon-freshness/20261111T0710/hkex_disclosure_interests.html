Detail companion to the HKEX Insider Disclosures dataset: the named parties behind each Part-XV (SDI) filing. Where HKEX Insider Disclosures carries only presence flags for the nested interest blocks, this dataset lists them — persons acting in concert, other parties to a s.317 agreement, controlled corporations, joint shareholders, trusts, the capacity in which shares are held, and derivative interests — each with its party names, codes and long/short share counts. One row per disclosure form; join to HKEX Insider Disclosures on form_serial_number. Multi-valued blocks are flattened to single, pipe-delimited (" | ") cells, aligned by element index across a block's columns. Dependent (child/spouse) deemed interests are included, as HKEX's own mandatory SDI filing already discloses them publicly.
