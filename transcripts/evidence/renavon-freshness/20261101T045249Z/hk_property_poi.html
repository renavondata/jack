Hong Kong points of interest from several sources, one row per amenity record: the Centaline property portal's amenity graph, and Hong Kong government open data — Hospital Authority hospitals, LCSD sports centres and parks, public libraries, Transport Department ferry piers, the Lands Department's iGeoCom GeoCommunity Database (convenience stores, supermarkets, markets, cooked-food stalls, clinics, bus termini, MTR entrances, kindergartens and more) and the FEHD register of licensed restaurants. Near-duplicates across sources are removed, and iGeoCom's several records for one premises collapse to one amenity. Each point carries a category (Transport, Parking, Daily convenience, Food & drink, Health, Education, Recreation), a finer sub_type, bilingual names, WGS84 coordinates and, where the source gives one, a bilingual street address. Pair it with the coordinates in hk_property_buildings to measure what is near a building.
