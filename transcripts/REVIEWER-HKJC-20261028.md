## Findings on `world.hkjc` terms in `transcripts/world-types.md`
1. **Ambiguity between "settled flat return" and advised-stake return (plus `VOID` refunds)**: `world-types.md` calls the quantity *"settled flat return"* (matching the 1-unit-per-pick `won * odds - 1` base rate in `HKJC-EPROCESS-20261028.md` and `roi_flat` on `/proofing`), but defines it parenthetically as *"the sum over those rows of (stake × WIN dividend per dollar if the runner won, else zero) minus the sum of stakes, as the ledger settles it"*, which is the variable 1u/2u/3u advised-stake return (`roi_advised`). Those two signs can disagree on a meeting: on `HV_20261021` (4 picks), the advised-stake net return in `ledger.sqlite` is `+0.80u` (one 2u winner at 2.90 paying 5.80u against 5.0u total stake) whereas the 1u flat return is `-1.10u` (`2.90 - 4.00`). Moreover, for a scratched non-starter (`result_status = 'VOID'`), `data/settle_picks.py` records `payout = stake_units` (a refund, net 0), whereas the parenthetical *"if the runner won, else zero"* would treat a refunded scratching as a full loss (`-stake`) unless *"as the ledger settles it"* (`sum(payout - stake_units)`) governs.
2. **Evidence surface on `https://hkjc.guru/proofing` (`limit=50`)**: `app/routes/proofing.py` renders only cumulative summary metrics and the 50 most recently settled picks (`get_recent_settled_picks(limit=50)`), with no per-meeting aggregation table; if a meeting is resolved after more than 50 subsequent picks have settled, its rows will have rolled off the HTML page even though `ledger.sqlite` retains them.
