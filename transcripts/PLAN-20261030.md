## 1. What we are optimising for
Discounted value: outcomes in priced worlds, and information with a price (what a measurement would
change), discounted by time, net of the sovereign's hours, the money budget (seq 50) and compute;
rigour — every claim scored, every rule witnessed, nothing irreversible unpriced — is a constraint,
not a term. "Profitable" means discounted, not short-term. The discount rate is a constant for the
admitted list; it is set when a decision first needs it, not before. Speed matters only as the
discount; the programme's own measure of speed is the time to the first scored decision taken by an
agent with positive realised value in a priced world — presently undefined, because no agent decides.

## 3. Two rules about time
- **Pipelining.** The next layer's *pre-stated* deliverables are built during the current layer's
  window; the window opens the day the previous one closes. Permitted because BRIEF-C1 and §8 already
  name C1b's and C2's content; forbidden for C3, whose brief does not exist (Gall).
- **Windows are maxima** (A5, one clause): a window ends at the earlier of its stated end and a
  `decision` of the sovereign closing it, enterable once the window's pre-stated criteria hold on
  the current prefix; the exit prefix is the log at that instant. Forced by: C1a met on 29 Oct,
  idle until 8 Nov by A3's text. In C1b's brief the general rule: a window's end is the promotion
  forecast's `resolves_by`, no other constant.

## 5. The loop by hand (operating procedure until the clerk and the election are code)
1. State: log and clock; obligations computed each morning (overdue forecasts, open proposals,
   window criteria, ratio) — by hand until `jack status`.
2. Invite: who proposes next is chosen by record and the choice is written in the invitation — by
   hand until Hedge.
3. Propose with a forecast: every proposal carries its author's forecast of acceptance and effect
   (assize's `pins_green` / `review_accept` / `merged` types), which is C2's question set for free.
4. Judge: validity by the kernel, admission by the judge; never by opinion.
5. Resolve and score: the herald, the evidence file, `jack score`.
6. Change the loop only through the loop: every amendment carries its forecast (A4: seq 60); every
   recommendation to the sovereign is a forecast of his answer (item 13).
