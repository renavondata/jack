### Row 68: verified, with an important qualification

At `6c0566c3`, through seq 5564, using the stated attack instant `2026-11-05T11:23:23Z`, I reproduced:

- **1,524 events**
- Actual mixture **E = 7.6386243 × 10³²**, first crossing at **event 53**
- Mean log-loss: pooled base **0.6920127**; actual mixture **0.2986670**
- Per-dataset Laplace frequency, updated on every prior resolution: **E = 2.0054365 × 10³³**, first crossing at **event 50**; mean loss **0.2950398**
- Actual mixture against that per-dataset comparator: **E = 0.9145075**

That confirms the central numerical finding in `transcripts/COUNSEL-OPEN.md:77`. F is an additional, weaker construction: a fixed dataset-name lookup also passes through the actual mixture calculation.

**“Measures nothing” is too strong.** F5 already stated the defensible conclusion: the test has the scope of its pooled comparator, permits cadence information that comparator omits, and does not establish general model superiority (`transcripts/REVIEW-C2a-v2.md:210–229`). A successful no-model baseline does not invalidate the e-process algebra. It defeats its use as a discriminator of the stronger skill claim.

The retrospective per-dataset diagnostic also must not silently become a forecast issued before a cutoff. Its resolution-prefix calculation and an executable pre-cutoff forecaster are different objects. F avoids that timing problem: its probabilities are fixed before any event.

Strictly, this is not an event beyond *every possible* party control: opening coordination, branch admission and the ancestor predicate are designed mechanisms. It is an opening/merge race that ordinary participants cannot retrospectively cure. Calling every such race “outside every party’s control” would obscure the mechanism responsible.

### Several requested implications should not be assumed

- **No-skill passage does not automatically make an administrative safety criterion empty.** A genuinely killed finite mutant suite, a physical-line bound, and absence of a veto can be sound literal conditions without measuring forecasting skill.
- **Current C2b criterion 7 is not the original 24-round criterion.** Amendment 10 expressly makes it a build test and removes live election exercise, B:685–689. Calling zero live rounds a false pass of its current wording would be wrong. It does leave the earlier “election that acts” purpose, B:46–55, unestablished.
- **The C2c-1 JSON file governs.** C:7 says differences from its prose are findings, not grounds for preferring the prose. Its twelve criteria—not every R-rule or every future-window promise—are the twelve audited here.
- **No finite twin/mutant suite proves universal semantic adequacy.** Conversely, discovering an uncovered possibility is not evidence that an existing implementation actually exhibits it. The C2c findings are specification-level constructions; this branch does not contain its proposed `tools/window.py`.
